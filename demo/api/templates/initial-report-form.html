State law requires mandated reporters to immediately make a report to the child welfare agency when they have reasonable cause to believe that a child under the age of 18 is suffering from abuse and/or neglect by:

STEP 1: Immediately reporting by oral communication to the local agency intake office (see contact information at end of form); and

STEP 2: Completing and sending this written Initial Report to the local agency office within 48 hours of making the oral report.

For more information about requirements for mandated reporters and filing a report of alleged abuse and/or neglect, refer to your agency's mandated reporter guide.
